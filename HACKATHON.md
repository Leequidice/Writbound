# Writbound hackathon brief

**Autonomous work. Binding accountability.**

Writbound demonstrates a local Ollama CSV worker, independent deterministic verification, and a deployed NEAR testnet escrow. Job 1 paid the worker for verified output after its full challenge window. Job 2 refunded the customer after challenge 0 was upheld against an intentionally defective fixture with a deliberately seeded positive attestation. Both transfer callbacks finalized.
